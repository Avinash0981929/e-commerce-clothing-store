Our Story

Where Elegance Meets Fashion

Elegant Threads was founded with a passion for celebrating the beauty and grace of women through fashion. We believe every woman deserves to feel confident and elegant, whether she is attending a grand wedding, a corporate meeting, or a casual brunch with friends.

Our carefully curated collection spans traditional Indian wear like Banarasi silk sarees and intricate lehengas to contemporary western dresses and fusion wear. Every piece in our collection is handpicked for its quality, craftsmanship, and timeless appeal.

Located in the heart of Bangalore on the iconic Brigade Road, our boutique offers a premium shopping experience where our expert stylists help you find the perfect outfit for every occasion.
